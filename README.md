## 🌟 Core Highlights & Business Features

- ⚡ **100/100 Lighthouse Score:** Zero heavy frameworks or runtime overhead. Raw semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- 📱 **Mobile-First Responsive Design:** Seamlessly adapts across smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide monitors.
- 🛒 **Interactive Checkout & Ordering Modal:** Dynamic cart calculations, item customization, and multiple payment methods:
  - 📱 **bKash & Nagad** (Instant mobile banking routing)
  - 💳 **Credit / Debit Cards** (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
  - 💵 **Cash on Delivery**
- 💬 **Direct WhatsApp Business Bridge:** Instant order dispatch formatting order items, customer address, and total amount into a structured WhatsApp message.
- 📍 **Interactive Location & Inquiries:** Branded Google Maps embed, real-time opening hours, and direct contact form.
- 🔒 **Enterprise Hygiene & Zero DOM-XSS:** Stringent sanitization (`textContent` + `encodeURIComponent`), secured external links with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
